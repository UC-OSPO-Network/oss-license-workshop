::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: challenge

## Myth or Fact about Open Source Software (OSS)

Instructions: Mark each statement as a Myth or a Fact.

1. [Myth or Fact] If you post your code publicly on the internet, it automatically becomes Open Source.
2. [Myth or Fact] An open source license is essentially a legal "permission slip" from the creator.
3. [Myth or Fact] An open source license means I am giving away my ownership of the code.

:::::::::::::::: solution

1. Myth. Default copyright/IP law applies; public does not mean permission
2. Fact. An OS license tells users exactly what they are allowed to do with the intellectual property.
3. Myth. A license does not give away ownership. It is simply a legal document where you (the owner) grant specific permissions to other people to use your code.
