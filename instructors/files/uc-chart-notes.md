# Notes: UC OSS Chart summary in Episode 3

Episode 3 (`episodes/when-license-clash.md`) summarizes the University of California Office of the President OSS Chart (4/17/18 version) in a few bullets and links to the chart. An earlier version transcribed the full tables. Tim chose the link and summary on 2026-11-11, because the UCOP terms of use give no general permission to reuse its documents.

* Chart: https://security.ucop.edu/files/documents/resources/oss-chart.pdf
* Companion: https://security.ucop.edu/files/documents/resources/oss-chart-companion.pdf
* Guide: https://security.ucop.edu/files/documents/resources/guide-to-managing-open-source-software.pdf

Checks on 2026-11-11:

* The UCOP terms page (https://www.ucop.edu/terms/index.html) says site content is owned by The Regents of the University of California. It states no reuse permission or open license.
* The UCOP resources page says the tools are "available for use within and the benefit of the UC system" and that Locations may "distribute and tailor the documents". Our lesson is public under CC BY 4.0, so we do not rely on that.
* The live chart, companion, and guide PDFs match the copies we used byte for byte. The page lists no newer version and no dates.
* Contacts for campus copyright decisions are at https://copyright.universityofcalifornia.edu/resources/permissions.html (not read yet).

Open points:

* If someone wants the full tables back, ask the UC copyright office for permission first.
* The documents date from 2018.
* The chart rates risk under UC policy. It is not a compatibility chart. The cohort still has to write the compatibility content for Episode 3.
