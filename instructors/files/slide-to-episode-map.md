## Points to settle when we step through

* Episode 3 has two bullets of slide content and no clash example. The group needs to write most of it.
* Slide 7 splits across Episodes 1 and 4. The first sentence is about why licenses help. The second introduces ownership.
* Ownership (slide 8) and TTO (slide 9) come before the license types in the slides. The draft moves them to Episode 4. This supports the capstone question of who approves a release.
* Slide 13 has the LGPL linking example. It fits Episode 2 (weak vs strong copyleft) and Episode 3 (interaction with other code).
* Slide 12 has a reviewer marker, `[KP1]`. Its author and meaning are unknown. The draft keeps it as an HTML comment.
* The challenge on slide 18 assumes a live demo repository and TTO approval. It is written for a facilitated workshop. Learners working alone have no TTO to ask.
* The slides have no exercises for Episodes 1 to 3. Existing exercises in `episodes/` are not touched.
* The slides carry no figures. The slide 11 spectrum is text only.
* Slide 17 resource links were not in the text export.
